# Taxonomy

This file defines the organizing categories used in the glossary.

## 1. Core AI governance

Used for terms concerning oversight, accountability, and controls for AI systems.

- model governance
- risk management
- human oversight
- explainability
- transparency
- auditability

## 2. Data protection and privacy

Includes legal concepts around personal data processing, consent, rights, and regulators.

- personal data
- data minimization
- legitimate interest
- data protection authority
- right to erasure
- automated decision-making

## 3. Platform and digital market regulation

Applies to online platforms, intermediary liability, and competition concerns.

- platform accountability
- notice-and-takedown
- systemic risk
- market power
- interoperability
- algorithmic ranking

## 4. Liability and responsibility

Terms used to allocate legal responsibility for harms caused by AI and digital services.

- product liability
- strict liability
- duty of care
- compensation
- fault-based liability
- attributable harm

## 5. Rights and procedural safeguards

Defines rights and procedural protections relevant to individuals and affected groups.

- due process
- right to explanation
- contestability
- redress
- access to information
- non-discrimination

## 6. Emerging technology and cross-border concepts

Covers concepts relevant to new technologies, regulatory gaps, and inter-jurisdictional issues.

- AI Act
- foundation model
- generative AI
- synthetic data
- frontier model
- tech neutrality

## Jurisdiction mapping

- EU: applicable to institutions, directives, regulations, and cross-border market rules
- French law: national implementation, administrative decisions, and sector-specific rules
- US law: federal and state frameworks, sectoral regulation, and common law concepts

## Recommended tagging

Each glossary row should include:

- term
- jurisdiction
- category
- definition
- status (proposed, in force, or guidance)
- source_url

This structure allows the glossary to support legal comparison across jurisdictions without losing precision.
