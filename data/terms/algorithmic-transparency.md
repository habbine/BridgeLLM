---
term: Algorithmic Transparency
jurisdiction: EU
category: Core AI governance
definition: The practice of making the logic, decision factors, and relevant constraints of automated systems understandable for oversight and accountability.
status: emerging
source_url: https://www.oecd.org/going-digital/ai/
---

Algorithmic transparency is relevant where automated systems materially affect individuals or organizations. It may involve disclosure of model characteristics, decision criteria, or meaningful information about how and why a result was produced.
