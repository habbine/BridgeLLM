---
term: General Data Protection Regulation
jurisdiction: EU
category: Data protection and privacy
definition: Regulation governing personal data processing, rights, and accountability in the European Union.
status: in force
source_url: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
---

The GDPR sets rules for the collection, processing, and transfer of personal data. It creates rights for individuals and obligations for controllers and processors, including accountability, data minimization, and security.
