---
term: AI Act
jurisdiction: EU
category: Core AI governance
definition: EU regulation establishing a harmonized framework for AI systems based on risk and obligations.
status: in force
source_url: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
---

The EU AI Act introduces a risk-based framework for artificial intelligence systems. It distinguishes between prohibited practices, high-risk systems, and lower-risk systems, and imposes transparency duties and governance requirements for providers and deployers.
