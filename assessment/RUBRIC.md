# Scoring guide

One page. It tells you how to answer the questions on the assessment form.

## What you are assessing

For each term, answer for the jurisdictions you know. **Skip the ones you do not know** ("Don't know" is a fine answer). Judge the law as it stands on the day you assess.

- **EU**: EU regulations, directives and decisions, and the case law of the EU courts.
- **France**: French statutes, regulations and case law, including EU law as transposed.
- **US**: federal and state law. If states differ, say so in your assessment.

"Law" means binding rules and court decisions. Guidelines, codes of practice and standards are not law for scoring, but cite them as sources.

## 1. How much law covers the term? (per jurisdiction)

| Score | Meaning |
|:--:|:--|
| **0** | **Specifically regulated.** A legal text names or defines the concept, or regulates it directly. |
| **1** | **Regulated in part.** Specific rules exist, but they cover only some uses, actors or situations. |
| **2** | **Only general rules apply (indirect).** No rule targets the concept. General law (data protection, consumer, product liability, contract, tort and so on) reaches it only indirectly. |
| **3** | **No legal framework.** You cannot identify any law that applies. |
| **n/a** | **Technical term; no legal framework needed.** Use this for terms that do not call for legal treatment. |

If you hesitate between two scores, choose the higher number and say why. This keeps scores comparable between reviewers.

## 2. Timing of the rules

Pick one: rules apply today, rules adopted but not yet applicable, only proposed, mixed across jurisdictions (explain), or n/a.

Timing is separate from the score on purpose. A rule that is adopted but not yet applicable still counts as regulation in question 1; question 2 records that it does not bite yet.

## 3. Does the legal definition match how the technology works?

Matches · Partly matches · Does not match · No legal definition exists · Don't know

Coverage is not quality. A term can score 0 in question 1 and still be defined in a way that does not match the technology. Question 3 is where you say so.

## Always

- **Add a source for every score.** Give the article, paragraph, case or page. A score without a source cannot be checked, and we may set it aside.
- Explain disagreements with the wording of a term in "Your assessment".
- Assess before you read other assessments of the same term.
